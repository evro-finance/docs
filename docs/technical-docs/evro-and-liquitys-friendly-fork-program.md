# Evro & Liquity's Friendly Fork Program

We are a “Friendly Fork” of Liquity V2 and chose to pay a small license fee to them and have been granted the exclusive right to use Liquity's code on Gnosis Chain. There’s a large overlap between Evro and Nerite’s teams — we’ve received enormous help directly from the Liquity team:

- They gave us lots of technical support from incredible engineers like Colin Platt.
- They shared their audit reports with us early, which allowed us to improve the security of our protocol faster.
- They helped us connect and partner with LP networks like the DeFi Collective.
- We also have to mention that without the support and interest of the Gnosis Community, Evro also wouldn't be possible.

We chose this design because it gives power to the people, is trustless, and contains no gotchas or perverse incentives. It’s an honor to be part of this program.

You can learn more about the Liquity Friendly Fork Program [on the Liquity Docs](https://docs.liquity.org/v2-documentation/friendly-fork-program).
